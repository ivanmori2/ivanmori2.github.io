A battlefield for your stories

WikiMockup was created to let alternate-history writers create infoboxes for their alternate timelines.

QUICK GUIDE

How to use the editor

✦
  1. Fill in the event details. Optional fields left blank will not appear in the preview.
  2. Choose an image from your device or paste a web address. Images are scaled without cropping.
  3. For the infobox image, try 220–300 px wide; preserve its aspect ratio and avoid stretching it.
  4. Add links with <a href="https://…">text</a>, line breaks with <br>, and flags with <img src="…" width="15px">. You can also select text and highlight it blue.
  5. Rename section headings and add custom rows. Apply changes, copy the HTML, or download a high-resolution image.
